FavSpeedDial

A Chrome extension that replaces your New Tab page with a fast, highly customizable speed dial based on your own browser bookmarks.

Features

  • Bookmark tiles: your bookmarks and folders as tiles with favicons, preview images or your own images, colors and titles.
  • Folder tabs and drag & drop: organize bookmarks directly on the dashboard.
  • Profiles: separate setups for work and private use, synced through Chrome Sync.
  • Widgets: clock, weather, notes, to-do lists, RSS feeds, stock ticker and quotes.
  • Search: multiple search engines with suggestions and a private, local search history.
  • Backup: export and import your settings as a file.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you turn it on in the settings, FavSpeedDial syncs custom tile images, background images and large profiles across your devices through your own Google Drive. It uses only the drive.appdata permission: a hidden folder that belongs to the extension. FavSpeedDial cannot see your Drive files, and no data is sent to the developer.

Privacy

FavSpeedDial has no developer server, no tracking and no advertising. Details: Privacy Policy.
