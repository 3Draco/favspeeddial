Privacy Policy for the FavSpeedDial browser extension
Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Contact

Questions about this policy or the extension: Sven.Draco@gmail.com

The developer does not process any personal data of users (see section 2). This address is for contact only.

2. Summary

FavSpeedDial does not collect any personal data for the developer. There is no developer server, no analytics or tracking, no advertising, and no data is sold or shared. Your bookmarks, settings and images stay in your browser or in your own Google account.

3. Storage in the browser

Settings, profiles, tile customizations (titles, colors, image addresses, note and to-do texts), widget settings and images are stored in your browser (chrome.storage, IndexedDB). Part of this is synced to your other devices through Chrome Sync of your Google account if you have enabled it in Chrome. Chrome Sync is operated by Google; the developer has no access to it.

4. Optional Google Drive sync

Only if you turn on "Enable Google Drive sync" in the settings and sign in with Google:

  • Scope: The extension requests only the drive.appdata permission. It can only read and write its own application folder in your Google Drive, which is hidden from you. It has no access to your files and folders in Google Drive.
  • Data transferred: the settings of all profiles listed above, tile customizations, widget settings (e.g. the location of a weather widget), locally stored background images and tile images you uploaded.
  • Purpose: solely syncing between your own devices and keeping a backup in your Google Drive.
  • Recipient: only Google (as the provider of Google Drive), in your own account. No data is transferred to the developer or to third parties.
  • Sign-in: The access token from Google is stored only locally in the browser and is sent only to Google.
  • Turning off and deleting: Turn off the switch in the settings. Delete the stored data in Google Drive (web) under Settings → Manage apps → FavSpeedDial → Options → Delete hidden app data. Revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

FavSpeedDial's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Requests to third-party services

For some features the extension requests data from third-party services directly from your browser. For technical reasons, the respective provider receives your IP address and the information listed. The developer receives none of it.

  • Website icons (favicons): Google (google.com/s2/favicons) and DuckDuckGo (icons.duckduckgo.com) receive the domain of the bookmark.
  • Preview images ("Automatic") and bookmark check: the bookmarked website receives a request for its address; Google Public DNS (dns.google) receives the domain of the bookmark.
  • Search suggestions: Google (suggestqueries.google.com) receives the typed search text.
  • Weather widget: Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) receives the entered location or coordinates.
  • Stock ticker: Yahoo Finance receives the selected symbols.
  • IP widget: ipify (api.ipify.org) is contacted to return your IP address.
  • RSS and calendar widgets: the feed and calendar addresses you entered are requested.

These requests only happen when you use the respective feature.

6. Your rights

Since the developer does not store any personal data about you, there is nothing to disclose or delete on the developer's side. You can delete all data yourself: by removing the extension (local data), in the Chrome Sync settings, and in Google Drive as described in section 4. For questions, contact the developer at the address in section 1.

7. Changes

If the processing of data changes, this policy will be updated and the date above changed.
